Generated by `scripts/run_eda_error_v2_1.py --model-name xgboost --selected-group F3` using the frozen V2.1 champion configuration (`configs/best_v2_1/xgboost_F3_final.yaml`), OOF validation predictions on `2024-01..12` (`data/interim/v2_1_champion_oof_2024.parquet`), and frozen forward-month evaluations across `2025-02..08` (excluding held-out `2025-09` and prospective `2025-10..11`).

## 1. Development Data Quality & Sampling Parity (`2023-01..2025-08`)

Source artifact: `reports/eda_v2_1/data_quality_summary.json`

- **Row & month coverage**: `3,200,000` sampled development rows spanning `32` months (`2023-01` through `2025-08`, `100,000` rows per month).
- **Primary key integrity**: `0` duplicate `flight_key` records detected across all development partitions.
- **Stratified downsampling fidelity**: `max_abs_sampling_positive_rate_delta = 0.003216` between full BTS monthly base rates and sampled partitions.
- **Target prevalence regime swing**: Overall development mean `DepDel15 = 0.208764`, ranging from a low of `0.13661` (`2024-10`) to a peak of `0.29550` (`2024-07`) — more than a 2x shift in base rate across the calendar year.
- **Expected BTS missingness / post-outcome sparsity**: `cancellation_code` is 100% null (cancelled flights excluded or uncoded prior to outcome), and the five BTS delay-cause columns are 78.81% null (populated only when delayed, never used as `T-24h` inputs).

## 2. Monthly Discrimination & Prevalence Dynamics (`2024-01..2025-08`)

Source artifact: `reports/eda_v2_1/monthly_metrics.csv`

- Evaluated across `19` validation months (`12` OOF months in `2024` plus `7` forward months `2025-02..08`).
- Peak discrimination occurs during convective summer peaks (for example `2025-07`: prevalence `0.28498`, `PR-AUC = 0.4688`, `P@5% = 0.5726`).
- Autumn shoulder lows show correspondingly lower absolute PR-AUC due to lower base prevalence (for example `2024-11`: prevalence `0.14278`, `PR-AUC = 0.2178`, `P@5% = 0.2694`), demonstrating why PR-AUC must always be interpreted alongside monthly prevalence and lift over base rate.

## 3. Slice Behavior & Unseen Entity Generalization

Source artifact: `reports/eda_v2_1/per_slice_metrics.csv`

- **Unseen route generalization**: Flights on previously unseen routes (`unseen_0` slice, `n = 1,736` in validation) maintain clean ranking separation (`PR-AUC = 0.4227` vs `0.2460` slice prevalence), showing that airline, airport geography, distance, and departure-hour encodings transfer even when a specific origin-dest pair was absent in prior training months.
- **Time-of-day & carrier regimes**: Predicted risk rises monotonically from early morning banks into evening propagation banks, consistent with delay-chain dynamics across the NAS.

## 4. High-Confidence Error Taxonomy (`T-24h` Structural Ceiling)

Source artifact: `reports/eda_v2_1/high_conf_errors_anonymized.csv`

- **High-confidence False Positives** (`p_hat = 0.639..0.678`, actual `y = 0`): Heavily concentrated in evening/late-night departures (`18-21` and `22-23` local bins) on low-cost/high-utilization carriers (`F9`, `NK`, `B6`, `WN`) departing major hubs/leisure stations (`ATL`, `FLL`, `MCO`). When inbound aircraft turn smoothly or convective weather clears, these high-risk slots depart on time despite historical schedule stress.
- **High-confidence False Negatives** (`p_hat = 0.049..0.061`, actual `y = 1`): Concentrated in early-morning banks (`06-09` and `00-05` local bins) involving carriers such as `WN` and `YX` at spokes/focus cities (`PIT`, `IND`, `AUS`, `LAS`). At `T-24h`, early morning schedules have the highest historical on-time reliability, so sudden mechanical faults, crew legality disruptions, or overnight ATC ground programs remain fundamentally unobservable 24 hours ahead.

## 5. Rolling Recalibration Audit

Source artifact: `reports/eda_v2_1/rolling_recalibration_check.csv`

- Fitting an online Platt recalibrator on the immediately preceding month reduces ECE during sustained summer regimes (`2025-06`: `0.0249 -> 0.0154`, `2025-07`: `0.0246 -> 0.0148`, `2025-08`: `0.0130 -> 0.0112`).
- However, it *worsens* calibration during regime transitions when the previous month's base rate lags the current month (`2025-03`: `0.0168 -> 0.0321`, `2025-05`: `0.0127 -> 0.0298`).
- **Recommendation**: Keep frozen base calibration in the static bundle for stability across shoulder transitions, while monitoring monthly ECE in `scripts/monitor.py` so seasonal recalibration or retraining triggers fire only when drift persists across consecutive windows.
